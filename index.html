ABM Flood Simulation

Agent-based flood simulation for the Kelani River Basin using GIS data and hydrodynamic modelling. Extended Mesa with a custom 3D MeshSpace — a capability absent from all existing open-source ABM platforms.

  • Processed 129 GIS coordinates across 248 triangular terrain faces
  • O(N) linear time complexity — 100 agents in 0.06s vs 2–10 min for HEC-RAS
  • Accepted for presentation at IEEE ICEEET 2026 — "Terrain-Aware ABM with 3D Delaunay Mesh Visualization in MESA: Kithulgala Sub-Basin, Sri Lanka"
